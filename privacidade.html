Privacy Policy — Cortes Sem Lei App

Last updated: November 19, 2026. Versão em português abaixo.

YouTube API Services

Cortes Sem Lei App uses the YouTube API Services. By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy) also applies to the data accessed through YouTube API Services.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Cortes Sem Lei, on the owner's own computer, to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Information accessed, collected and used

  • youtube.upload: to upload the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions and custom thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel name and the channel's own list of uploaded videos (titles), only to avoid uploading the same video twice.

The app does not access e-mail, contacts, viewing history or any data of other YouTube users. No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer (local file). The list of already-published titles is cached locally for up to 10 minutes. No YouTube data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deletion and revoking access

Disconnecting the account in the app ("Desconectar YouTube") deletes the stored token immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, all locally stored API data (token and cached titles) is deleted within 7 days at the latest; cached titles expire after 10 minutes in any case. To request deletion, contact us through the channel.

Contact

YouTube channel: Cortes Sem Lei.

O Cortes Sem Lei App usa os Serviços de API do YouTube. Ao usar o app, você concorda com os Termos de Serviço do YouTube; a Política de Privacidade do Google também se aplica.
